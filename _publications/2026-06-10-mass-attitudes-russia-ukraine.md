Published online as Open Access (CC BY 4.0) on 10 June 2026. The paper asks why some Europeans hold ambivalent or pro-Kremlin views on Russia's invasion of Ukraine. Using an original survey of over 12,000 respondents across five countries and a 24,000-respondent replication with the Solidarity in Europe dataset, it finds that closeness to pro-Kremlin parties and exposure to disinformation explain more of this variation than ideology or economic self-interest.
